Matatu Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Matatu Rush ("the app") is made by JuicyOnion, Nairobi, Kenya. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (stage reached, coin balance, paint jobs, settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.

Game statistics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played, for example which stages players finish or find too hard, and to find crashes. Firebase may collect your device's app instance ID, device model, operating system, approximate country and in-game events. This information does not identify you by name and helps us make the game better. Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy something in the app (for example removing ads), payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm which purchases belong to your device so they can be restored; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase receipt. Learn more: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and on iOS the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier can be used for tracking.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).
  • iOS: turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Deleting the app removes all game progress stored on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
